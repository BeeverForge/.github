## Community

[**beeverforge-oss**](https://github.com/BeeverForge/beeverforge-oss) is the open-source core, under Apache-2.0. It runs on your machines, with no vendor lock-in: coding agents carry out the work, policies authorize every action, and people evaluate the results.

## Enterprise

The modules a company puts on top of the community core: accounts, roles, audit and intake across many products and teams. Every paid plan carries all of them.

## Security

Report a vulnerability privately through the repository's *Security* tab. Please do not open a public issue for it.
