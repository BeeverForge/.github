## Community

The open-source core. It runs on your machines, under Apache-2.0, with no vendor lock-in: a framework that runs software tickets autonomously with coding agents, where policies authorize actions and humans evaluate results.

## Enterprise

The modules a company puts on top of the community core: accounts, roles, audit and intake across many products and teams. Every paid plan carries all of them.

## Security

Report a vulnerability privately through the repository's *Security* tab. Please do not open a public issue for it.
